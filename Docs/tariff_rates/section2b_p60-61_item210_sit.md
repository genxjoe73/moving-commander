# Item 216 — Carrier Supervisory Personnel (page 61)

When requested in writing by shipper, carrier provides supervisory personnel at origin/destination.

| Hours | Per Hour, Per Man |
|---|---|
| Regular Hours or Days | $109.00 |
| Overtime Hours or Days | $174.25 |

- **Regular hours:** 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m., Monday–Friday (excluding holidays).
- **Overtime hours:** 5:00 p.m.–8:00 a.m. weekdays, plus all day Saturday/Sunday/holidays.
