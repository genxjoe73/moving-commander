# MAX 4 Tariff — Extracted Rules

Source: `docs/2021_MAX_4_Tariff.pdf` (Southwest Movers Association, Texas, effective January 1, 2018)

## Scope
- Texas household-goods moves only (Texas Transportation Code Ch. 643).
- These are MAXIMUM rates. Carriers may discount from them.
- Cumulative surcharge cap: 30% (fuel/labor/etc.), Item F.

## Pricing Programs (Items 70, 71, 72)
- **Binding Proposal** — fixed price, signed in writing, 60-day validity.
- **Not To Exceed Proposal** — ceiling price; final based on actual weight, 60-day validity.
- **Pre-Existing Contract** — corporate/relocation contracts can override.

## Item 22 — Hourly Time Rounding
This is a billing-rounding rule, not a dollar rate.

| Time | Billed As |
|---|---|
| 0–15 min | 1/4 hour |
| 16–30 min | 1/2 hour |
| 31–45 min | 3/4 hour |
| 46–60 min | 1 hour |

Apply this rounding any time we bill an hourly tier in the Quick Quote.

## Section 3 — Governing Provisions (page 65)

Applies to non-local Texas household-goods shipments. Local shipments use Section 4.

- Rates are expressed in **dollars per shipment**, looked up by weight × distance.
- Charges include loading, unloading, and transportation. Additional services (Section 2) bill separately.
- Apply to actual weight + Item 130 weight additives, subject to minimum weights.
- When shipment is released to ≤ $0.60/lb/article, no Additional Valuation Charges. Otherwise, Item 190 valuation charges apply.

### Excess-distance addition (Note 4)
When distance exceeds the table's max, add per-100-miles (or fraction) by weight bracket:

| Weight Bracket | Per Each Add'l 100 Miles |
|---|---|
| 1,000–1,999 lbs | $88 |

| 12,000–15,999 lbs | $686 |
| 16,000+ lbs | $847 |

### Excess-weight addition (Note 4C)
For shipments over 23,999 lbs, add per-cwt (per 100 lbs or fraction) charge from the 23,999 lb rate column.

## Section 4 — Local Shipment Rates (page 75)

**Local shipment definition:** moves wholly within a single county, OR between two contiguous counties.

**Pricing model:** hourly only. No weight-based table.

**Maximum hourly rates:**

| Resource | Regular Time | Overtime |
|---|---|---|
| Supervisor / Foreman | $105.30/hr | $174.25/hr |
| Driver, Mover, Packer | $94.45/hr | $141.60/hr |
| Vehicle | $56.85/hr | $62.55/hr |
| Moving equipment (4-wheel dolly, book dolly, machine dolly, special equipment) | $3.25/hr each | (no overtime listed) |

**Quote formula (local, max):**
```
total = (foreman_count × foreman_hourly × hours)
      + (mover_count × mover_hourly × hours)
      + (vehicle_count × vehicle_hourly × hours)
      + (equipment_count × $3.25 × hours)
```

Apply Item 22 time rounding (¼/½/¾/full hour brackets per the 15-min steps).

Apply 30% surcharge cap (Item F) only when carrier costs justify it.

These are MAXIMUMS. Carrier may discount.
